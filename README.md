# Cybersecurity Portfolio: Security Operations & IT Audit

Welcome to my portfolio. I am currently completing an applied cybersecurity internship and hold the rank of Security Engineer 2 within The Cyber Range training environment. My focus is bridging the gap between technical threat hunting and enterprise risk management.

Leveraging over a decade of experience in corporate finance and internal controls, I approach cybersecurity through an investigative lens. Through my hands-on training, I specialize in validating technical controls, detecting anomalies within Microsoft Azure environments, and translating raw security telemetry into actionable business intelligence.

This repository serves as a master record of my live-environment investigations, Post-Incident Reports, and compliance documentation.
