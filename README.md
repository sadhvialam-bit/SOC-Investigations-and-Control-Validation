# Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)

Welcome to the Governance, Risk, and Compliance branch of my portfolio. This repository translates technical cybersecurity metrics into actionable business intelligence. It demonstrates my ability to manage risk, ensure compliance with corporate policies, and facilitate communication between technical security teams and business stakeholders.

## Branch Objective
To showcase process-driven IT governance. The projects here focus on the "why" and "when" of cybersecurity: assessing risk severity, tracking Service Level Agreement (SLA) compliance, drafting executive summaries, and navigating Change Advisory Board (CAB) workflows.

## Core Competencies Demonstrated
*   **Risk Assessment:** Analyzing raw vulnerability scan data to prioritize remediation based on CVSS scores and corporate policy SLAs.
*   **IT Governance:** Drafting and executing formal Change Advisory Board (CAB) emergency change requests, complete with User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and rollback parameters.
*   **Stakeholder Communication:** Bridging the gap between Security and Operations by authoring clear, data-backed pre-distribution emails and remediation recommendations.
*   **Compliance Auditing:** Generating pre- and post-remediation executive reports to prove regulatory and internal policy compliance.
*   **Lifecycle Management:** Establishing continuous monitoring schedules to maintain long-term security baselines.
